# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

This file is the template's. Fill every section the first time `miles-design-hub` sets the project up, from the kickoff and the design; an empty section is a question Claude will answer by guessing.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Disposable prototypes in `prototypes/`, one folder per prototype, with the design's tokens in `kit/tokens.css`. The comps are Paper frames (`DESIGN.md`, Shell, names the ones that hold the shell). The live product is built elsewhere and is not in this repo; `miles-port` carries an approved prototype into it.

## Users

Who uses the product, in what role, how often, and to what end. Then the secondary readers: who sees the output without opening the product.

## Product Purpose

What the product does, in two or three sentences a new engineer could repeat. What success looks like for a user, and what the repeat loop is.

## Positioning

What the product claims that its alternatives do not, in the product's own words.

## Operating Context

- The shell: the navigation items in order, and what sits at the foot.
- The object model: the nouns, what holds what.
- The journey, in order.
- Dense or sparse, which units, which appearance modes.
- Work in progress, with the feature slug under `docs/sprints/`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Terminology as the product writes it: the abbreviations, the series names, the tiers.
- UI copy conventions: case, numbers, missing values, units.
- Undecided product facts. Do not invent these; name who decides.
- Known product gaps to design around, not hide.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and company.
- Typeface, as shipped. Binding.
- Brand colours, by token.
- Voice: plain, specific, sentence case, no hype.

## Evidence on Hand

- The live product and who has access to what.
- Reference screenshots and where they are.
- Research and kickoff notes under `docs/research/`.
- What does not exist (testimonials, pricing, real figures), so nothing is presented as real that is not.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the answer.
2. Every number traces to its inputs.
3. Preserve the product's system; add controls only where the journey needs them.
4. Warn before side effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Colour never carries meaning alone. Everything works by keyboard. State the product's own additions here (print, grayscale, screen reader paths).
